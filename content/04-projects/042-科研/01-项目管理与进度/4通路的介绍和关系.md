## 1.1 阳离子抗菌肽（CAMP）抗性（KEGG map 01503）

### (a) _Salmonella_ 的主要细胞功能

KEGG CAMP 抗性通路聚焦于包膜重塑，以减少外膜的净负电荷，限制阳离子肽（宿主防御素、阳离子抗菌肽、多粘菌素）的结合与插入。_Salmonella_ 中的关键特征包括：

- **PhoP/PhoQ 和 PmrA/PmrB 两种组分系统**，能够感知低 Mg²⁺、酸性 pH 和 CAMP，并重新编程包膜。([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3417628/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "阳离子抗菌肽作为激活信号..."))
    
- **脂质 A 和核心 LPS 的修饰**
    
    - 向脂质 A 添加 4-氨基-L-阿拉伯糖（Ara 4 N）和磷酸乙醇胺（PEtN）可降低外膜的负电荷。([KEGG](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/map01503?utm_source=chatgpt.com "KEGG PATHWAY: map 01503"))
        
    - PagP、LpxO 和 WaaY/WaaP 进一步改变脂质 A 和内核的酰化和磷酸化，调节膜的刚性和电荷。([KEGG](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/map01503?utm_source=chatgpt.com "KEGG PATHWAY: map 01503"))
        
- **外排和包膜应激系统**，如噬菌体冲击蛋白（Psp）系统，在周质应激和 CAMP 暴露下共同诱导，以维持膜完整性及质子动力势。([生物预印本](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.16.649068v1.full.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Salmonella 的噬菌体冲击蛋白系统对于..."))
    

总体而言，CAMP 抗性是一个协调的包膜重塑程序，而非单一的“抗性基因”。

### (b) 对入侵和持久感染的贡献

**入侵阶段（黏膜进入）：**

- 在肠道黏膜，_Salmonella_ 遇到上皮来源的防御素；在哺乳动物中，中性粒细胞产生的 LL-37；在鸡中，黏膜β-防御素占主导。([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7112556/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Avian defensins - PMC - PubMed Central"))
    
- CAMP 直接**激活 PhoPQ 和 PmrAB**，进而上调 LPS 修饰和其他毒力基因，有效地利用宿主肽作为“危险信号”，宣布进入宿主组织。([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3417628/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "阳离子抗菌肽作为激活信号..."))
    
- 这些包膜变化通过初始瓶颈（如 Peyer 氏斑/M 细胞和肠道上皮）提高生存能力，使足够的细菌能够到达固有层和全身部位以建立感染。([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3167890/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "PhoP/Q 调控群对生存和..."))
    

**持久感染阶段（细胞内及组织中持久性）：**

- 在巨噬细胞内，_Salmonella_ 位于酸化且富集阳离子肽的空泡中。CAMP 依赖的 PhoPQ 和 PmrAB 激活是其在该微环境中存活和复制的核心，也与小鼠模型中的全身性感染相关。([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3167890/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "PhoP/Q 调控群对生存和..."))
    
- CAMP 诱导的包膜重塑减少对内源性宿主肽及多粘菌素类抗生素的敏感性，有助于在脾脏、肝脏和胆囊等组织中长期存活。([KEGG](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/map01503?utm_source=chatgpt.com "KEGG PATHWAY: map 01503"))
    

### (c) 逃避或对抗宿主防御

- **直接中和 CAMP 攻击：** 脂质 A 的 Ara 4 N/PEtN 修饰及改变的磷酸化可减少 CAMP 与膜的静电吸引，从而降低膜通透性。([KEGG](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/map01503?utm_source=chatgpt.com "KEGG PATHWAY: map 01503"))
    
- **包膜应激管理：** Psp 系统对于周质应激下的生存至关重要，并有助于在感染模型中对宿主抗菌因子（包括 CAMP）的耐受。([生物预印本](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.16.649068v1.full.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Salmonella 的噬菌体冲击蛋白系统对于..."))
    
- **免疫调节：** 改变的 LPS 结构可抑制 TLR 4 信号和补体激活，从而减少炎症损伤并提高生存能力。([ASM Journals](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jb.00318-24?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Only time will tell: lipopolysaccharide glycoform and biofilm ..."))

####  对入侵和持久感染的贡献

**上皮细胞入侵和早期细胞内生长：**

- 在上皮细胞中，_S. Typhimurium_ 高度依赖氧化磷酸化来生成 ATP；缺乏 ETC 组分或 ATP 合成酶的突变体会导致增殖和毒力受损。([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4773185/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "A Comparison of the ATP Generating Pathways Used by S ..."))

    - 能量消耗大的 T 3 SS 组装和分泌。
        
    - LPS、O-抗原（核苷糖）及其他包膜成分的生物合成。
        
    - 活跃的营养摄取和运动。

- 包括**精氨酸代谢**在内的代谢重编程支持抗氧化防御和 pH 稳态，有助于在氧化和酸性应激下维持存活能力。([ASM Journals](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/iai.00120-23/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Arginine Metabolism Powers Salmonella Resistance to ..."))

## 2.4 协同_Salmonella_ 策略的整合模型

将这些方面整合在一起，一个协调的生命周期策略逐渐清晰：

1. **进入宿主：**
    
    - CAMP 抗性和 O-抗原（来自核苷糖）确保通过富含 CAMP 的黏膜和生殖道表面。
        
2. **利用细胞内微环境：**
    
    - OxPhos 和活跃的核糖体在早期感染中提供入侵系统、应激防御和合成途径所需的能量和蛋白质合成能力。
        
3. **抵御宿主免疫和环境压力：**
    
    - 营养性免疫和氧化/包膜应激通过适应性改变 OxPhos、CAMP 抗性及 LPS 结构被抵消。
        
4. **转换为和退出持久性状态：**
    
    - 协调下调 OxPhos 和核糖体活性（加上 IbsA 等毒素）产生代谢活动低和抗原表达低的静止持久性菌体，具有长期生存能力。
        
    - 在条件改善时，核糖体被重新激活，ATP 水平上升，系统转换回入侵/复制模式。
        
在**鸡类宿主**中，该模块被调节以实现肠道和生殖道的持续定植以及蛋清中的生存，从而支持垂直传播。在**哺乳动物宿主**中，它支持巨噬细胞和组织中的系统感染和慢性携带。

从机制上看，这四个通路构成了一个**融合、可适应的核心模块**，将代谢、包膜结构及应激反应与入侵、存活、复制和持久性等表型联系起来，定义了_Salmonella_ 的致病过程。

## 1.1 Cationic Antimicrobial Peptide (CAMP) Resistance (KEGG map01503)

### (a) Primary cellular function in _Salmonella_

The KEGG CAMP resistance pathway centers on envelope remodeling to reduce the net negative charge of the outer membrane and limit the binding and insertion of cationic peptides (host defensins, cathelicidins, polymyxins). Key features in _Salmonella_ include:

- **Two-component systems PhoP/PhoQ and PmrA/PmrB** that sense low Mg²⁺, acidic pH, and CAMPs and reprogram the envelope.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3417628/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Cationic antimicrobial peptides serve as activation signals ..."))
    
- **Lipid A and core LPS modifications**
    
    - Addition of 4-amino-L-arabinose (Ara4N) and phosphoethanolamine to lipid A (arnBCADTEF, eptA/pmrC) decreases outer-membrane negative charge.([KEGG](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/map01503?utm_source=chatgpt.com "KEGG PATHWAY: map01503"))
        
    - PagP, LpxO and WaaY/WaaP further alter acylation and phosphorylation of lipid A and inner core, tuning membrane rigidity and charge.([KEGG](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/map01503?utm_source=chatgpt.com "KEGG PATHWAY: map01503"))
        
- **Efflux and envelope stress systems**, such as the phage shock protein (Psp) system, are co-induced under periplasmic stress and CAMP exposure to maintain membrane integrity and proton motive force.([生物预印本](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.16.649068v1.full.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The Salmonella phage shock protein system is required for ..."))
    

Overall, CAMP resistance is a coordinated envelope remodeling program, not a single “resistance gene”.

### (b) Contribution to invasion and persistent infection

**Invasion phase (mucosal entry):**

- At the intestinal mucosa, _Salmonella_ encounters epithelial-derived defensins and, in mammals, neutrophil CATHELICIDIN LL-37; in chickens, mucosal β-defensins dominate.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC7112556/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Avian defensins - PMC - PubMed Central"))
    
- CAMPs directly **activate PhoPQ and PmrAB**, which in turn up-regulate LPS-modification and other virulence genes, effectively using host peptides as “danger signals” that announce entry into host tissues.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3417628/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Cationic antimicrobial peptides serve as activation signals ..."))
    
- These envelope changes increase survival through the initial bottleneck at Peyer’s patches/M cells and intestinal epithelium, allowing enough bacteria to reach the lamina propria and systemic compartment to establish infection.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3167890/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Contribution of the PhoP/Q regulon to survival and ..."))
    

**Persistent infection phase (intracellular & tissue persistence):**

- Inside macrophages, _Salmonella_ resides in an acidified, cationic peptide–rich vacuole. CAMP-dependent activation of PhoPQ and PmrAB is central to survival and replication in this niche and to systemic disease in the mouse model.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3167890/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Contribution of the PhoP/Q regulon to survival and ..."))
    
- CAMP-induced envelope remodeling reduces sensitivity to endogenous host peptides as well as to polymyxin-like antibiotics, contributing to long-term persistence in tissues such as spleen, liver, and gall bladder.([KEGG](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/map01503?utm_source=chatgpt.com "KEGG PATHWAY: map01503"))
    

### (c) Evasion or counteraction of host defenses

- **Direct neutralization of CAMP attack:** lipid A modification with Ara4N/PEtN and altered phosphorylation reduce electrostatic attraction of CAMPs, decreasing membrane permeabilization.([KEGG](https://www.kegg.jp/entry/map01503?utm_source=chatgpt.com "KEGG PATHWAY: map01503"))
    
- **Envelope stress management:** the Psp system is required for survival under periplasmic stress and contributes to tolerance against host antimicrobial factors (including CAMPs) in infection models.([生物预印本](https://www.biorxiv.org/content/10.1101/2025.04.16.649068v1.full.pdf?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The Salmonella phage shock protein system is required for ..."))
    
- **Immunomodulation:** altered LPS structure can dampen TLR4 signaling and complement activation, thereby reducing inflammatory damage and enhancing survival.([ASM Journals](https://journals.asm.org/doi/10.1128/jb.00318-24?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Only time will tell: lipopolysaccharide glycoform and biofilm ..."))
    

### (d) Avian vs mammalian context

**Avian (chicken) host:**

- Chicken **heterophils lack a strong oxidative burst** and rely heavily on avian β-defensins and other CAMPs for rapid killing.([BioMed Central](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2164-9-526?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Gene expression profiling in chicken heterophils with ..."))
    
- _S. Enteritidis_ must withstand antimicrobial peptides in the **oviduct and egg white**, where CAMPs, lysozyme and ovotransferrin create a powerful antimicrobial milieu. Stress-induced survival strategies (likely including CAMP resistance and envelope stress responses) are implicated in persistent colonization of the oviduct and survival in egg albumen.([BioMed Central](https://gutpathogens.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1757-4749-2-23?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Stress-induced survival strategies enable Salmonella ..."))
    

**Mammalian host:**

- Neutrophil and epithelial **defensins** and **LL-37** are abundant in gut and systemic sites. LL-37 can activate innate immunity and damage bacteria, but _Salmonella_ can survive even in its presence; in human macrophages, LL-37 is not strictly required for bacterial clearance, implying redundancy of host defenses.([ASM Journals](https://journals.asm.org/doi/pdf/10.1128/iai.00672-12?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Cathelicidin Antimicrobial Peptide Expression Is Not Induced ..."))
    
- PhoPQ/PmrAB-mediated CAMP resistance is central to survival in mouse macrophages and systemic infection, making it a core determinant of mammalian virulence.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC3167890/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Contribution of the PhoP/Q regulon to survival and ..."))

## 1.2 Biosynthesis of Various Nucleotide Sugars (KEGG map00520)

### (a) Primary cellular function in _Salmonella_

KEGG “Biosynthesis of nucleotide sugars” encompasses the generation of activated sugars (e.g. UDP-Gal, UDP-GlcNAc, GDP-mannose, dTDP-rhamnose) used as building blocks of:

- **LPS core and O-antigen**
    
- **Capsular polysaccharides and exopolysaccharides (e.g. cellulose)**
    
- **Surface glycoproteins and other glycoconjugates**
    

In _Salmonella_, genes for O-antigen biosynthesis cluster together and fall into three main groups: (i) nucleotide sugar biosynthesis, (ii) glycosyltransferases, and (iii) O-unit processing/export.([ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/8963191_Biosynthesis_of_O-antigens_Genes_and_pathways_involved_in_nucleotide_sugar_precursor_synthesis_and_O-antigen_assembly?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Biosynthesis of O-antigens: Genes and pathways involved ..."))

### (b) Contribution to invasion and persistent infection

**LPS O-antigen and complement/serum resistance:**

- Long O-antigen chains are crucial for _S. Enteritidis_ to **evade complement** and to survive in vivo, highlighting the importance of a fully functional nucleotide sugar supply for LPS biosynthesis.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11106168/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Lipopolysaccharide with long O-antigen is crucial for ..."))
    
- Altering O-antigen repeat number via chain-length regulators (e.g. Wzz) modulates colonization, membrane permeability and susceptibility to antimicrobials.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9969949/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The effect of O-antigen length determinant wzz on ..."))
    

**Persistent colonization in poultry:**

- In chickens, **O-antigen repeat number** in _S. Enteritidis_ is specifically linked to colonisation of the reproductive tract, egg contamination, and survival in egg albumen.([PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23551176/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "O-antigen repeat number in Salmonella enterica serovar ..."))
    
- LPS of _S. Enteritidis_ is also required for persistent gastrointestinal infection in young poultry.([科学直接](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0378113504001804?utm_source=chatgpt.com "The O-antigen of Salmonella enterica serotype Enteritidis ..."))
    

**Biofilm and chronicity:**

- Nucleotide sugars fuel synthesis of extracellular polysaccharides such as cellulose. Inside macrophages, excessive cellulose production actually **reduces virulence**, promoting a biofilm-like, less invasive state.([PMC](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4413311/?utm_source=chatgpt.com "Salmonella promotes virulence by repressing cellulose ..."))
