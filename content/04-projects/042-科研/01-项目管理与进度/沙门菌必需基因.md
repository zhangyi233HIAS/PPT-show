_Rationale:_ The peptidoglycan enzymes (_murA_–_murF_, _mraY_, _murG_, _ftsI_, etc.) are classic essential genes needed to build the cell wall[journals.plos.org](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0268889#:~:text=34,Current%20Opinion%20in%20Microbiology). The lipopolysaccharide pathway (LpxA/LpxC…Lpt pathway) is likewise indispensable for outer membrane integrity[journals.plos.org](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0268889#:~:text=34,Current%20Opinion%20in%20Microbiology). ## Summary and Considerations摘要与考虑

In rich LB medium, **Salmonella enterica** (subsp. _enterica_, e.g., S. Typhimurium) requires roughly 300–350 genes to be intact for survival[bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-018-4986-1#:~:text=a%20powerful%20method%20to%20identify,seq%2C%20and%20confirmed%20that). These essential genes span core information processes (DNA replication, transcription, translation), cell envelope biogenesis (peptidoglycan, LPS, membrane), and key metabolic and homeostatic pathways (cofactor biosynthesis, chaperones, ATP generation). We presented a comprehensive list of such genes with their functions and pathways. It is important to note:在富含 LB 培养基中， **沙门氏菌**肠菌（肠菌亚种，如 S. Typhimurium）大约需要 300–350 个基因才能存活 [bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-018-4986-1#:~:text=a%20powerful%20method%20to%20identify,seq%2C%20and%20confirmed%20that)。这些关键基因涵盖核心信息过程（DNA 复制、转录、翻译）、细胞包膜生物发生（肽聚糖、LPS、膜）以及关键的代谢和稳态通路（辅因子生物合成、伴侣蛋白、ATP 生成）。我们展示了这类基因及其功能和途径的全面列表。需要注意的是：

- **Strain and Condition Specificity:** The essential gene set can vary with bacterial strain and growth conditions. The above list is specific to a laboratory strain in nutrient-rich broth. For example, some genes listed (like _metE_ or certain biosynthetic enzymes) might not be essential if the growth medium is supplemented with the end product. Conversely, a gene non-essential in LB might become essential in minimal media or in vivo infection (and vice versa)[journals.plos.org](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0268889#:~:text=were%20available%20for%20137%20of,scale%20metabolic%20network)[bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-018-4986-1#:~:text=iron%20concentration%3A%20iron,On%20the%20contrary%2C%20the%20215). Essentiality here is defined for in vitro growth without additional supplements.**应变与病症特异性：** 基本基因集会根据细菌株和生长条件而变化。以上列表针对实验室培养的营养丰富高汤菌株。例如，某些列出的基因（如 _metE_ 或某些生物合成酶）如果培养基中加入最终产物，可能并非必需。相反，LB 中非必需的基因可能在微小培养基或体内感染中变得必需（反之亦然 [）journals.plos.org](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0268889#:~:text=were%20available%20for%20137%20of,scale%20metabolic%20network)[bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-018-4986-1#:~:text=iron%20concentration%3A%20iron,On%20the%20contrary%2C%20the%20215)。这里的必需性定义为体外生长，无需额外补充剂。
    
- **Redundancy and Compensation:** Salmonella often has duplicate or analogous functions for some processes (e.g., two D-Ala-D-Ala ligases, two EF-Tu genes, two PBP1 enzymes). In such cases, we consider the _function_ essential even if any one gene can be knocked out. We highlighted where at least one of a pair is required. Inactivation of both duplicates is lethal[bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2164-13-212#:~:text=We%20ranked%20all%20Salmonella%20genes,one%20gene%2C%20folA%2C%20was%20not).**冗余与薪酬：** 沙门氏菌在某些过程中常具有重复或类似的功能（例如，两个 D-Ala-D-Ala 连接酶，两个 EF-Tu 基因，两个 PBP1 酶）。在这种情况下，我们认为即使任何一个基因都可能被敲除，该_功能_也是必不可少的。我们标明了至少需要一对的部分。两个复制体同时失效是致命[bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2164-13-212#:~:text=We%20ranked%20all%20Salmonella%20genes,one%20gene%2C%20folA%2C%20was%20not)的。
    
- **Experimental Validation:** The list is derived from high-throughput knockout libraries (e.g., transposon insertion sequencing) and curated databases[bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-018-4986-1#:~:text=a%20powerful%20method%20to%20identify,phenotypic%20analysis%20of%20289%20essential)[journals.plos.org](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0268889#:~:text=using%20Database%20of%20Essential%20Genes,The%20genes%20falsely). Not every gene has been individually confirmed by a clean deletion, but a substantial subset have been validated (e.g., by constructing conditional mutants)[researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Genetic-footprinting-of-known-nonessential-and-essential-genes-Results-are-plotted-as_fig2_12169842#:~:text=of%20498%20fragments%20resulting%20in,set%20of%2015%20putative%20indispensable). False negatives/positives can occur in screens, so some genes on the border of essentiality might be re-classified with further testing[bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2164-13-212#:~:text=%E2%80%9Chighly%20selected%E2%80%9D%20genes,21).**实验验证：** 该列表来源于高通量敲除库（例如转座子插入测序）和 [journals.plos.org](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0268889#:~:text=using%20Database%20of%20Essential%20Genes,The%20genes%20falsely) 的策划数据库 [bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/s12864-018-4986-1#:~:text=a%20powerful%20method%20to%20identify,phenotypic%20analysis%20of%20289%20essential)。并非每个基因都通过干净的缺失单独确认，但相当一部分基因已被验证（例如通过构造条件突变体）[researchgate.net](https://www.researchgate.net/figure/Genetic-footprinting-of-known-nonessential-and-essential-genes-Results-are-plotted-as_fig2_12169842#:~:text=of%20498%20fragments%20resulting%20in,set%20of%2015%20putative%20indispensable)。筛查中可能出现假阴性或假阳性，因此一些处于本质边缘的基因可能会通过进一步检测[bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com](https://bmcgenomics.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1471-2164-13-212#:~:text=%E2%80%9Chighly%20selected%E2%80%9D%20genes,21)重新分类。
    
- **Implications for Drug Targets:** Essential genes, especially those with no human homolog (e.g., MurA-F, LpxC, FolA, etc.), are attractive antibiotic targets.
