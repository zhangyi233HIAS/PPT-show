## 二、每个基因的中文要点概述 + 典型文献（英文）

### 1. **gmhB（yaeD）—— LPS 内核肝庚糖通路的磷酸酶**

- **通路角色**：  
    gmhB 属于保守的 ADP-肝庚糖合成通路（GmhA–HldE–GmhB–RfaD）的一部分，为 LPS 内核肝庚糖提供前体，是 **沙门菌和大肠杆菌**等肠杆菌科细菌构建 LPS 内核的保守步骤之一。
    
- **沙门菌中的表型**：  
    在 **S. Typhimurium** 中，gmhB 的自发或工程突变会改变 LPS 内核结构，从而改变外膜通透性，对某些小分子抗感染化合物和抗生素（如 D66、novobiocin、erythromycin）出现耐受性或敏感性变化。
    
- **与宿主免疫的联系**：  
    ADP-heptose 作为这一通路的中间产物，已经被证明是一种新的 **先天免疫 PAMP**，可通过宿主的 ALPK1 信号通路诱导炎症反应；gmhB 与 rfaD 共同控制 ADP-heptose 的丰度和流向，因此在 **LPS 结构**和**免疫激活信号**之间起到关键枢纽作用。
    

**可引用的代表性文献（示例）：**

- Allgood SC et al., 2025, _Microorganisms_  
    “Mutations in genes with a role in cell envelope biosynthesis confer resistance to a small-molecule anti-infective in _Escherichia coli_ and _Salmonella Typhimurium_.”
    
- 关于 LPS 肝庚糖通路的综述与结构工作：  
    如 Laekas-Hameder M et al., 2024, _Journal of Bacteriology_, 讨论了 GmhA/HldE/GmhB/RfaD 等肝庚糖代谢酶与 LPS 糖型和生物被膜形成的关系。
    

---

### 2. **cydB——末端呼吸链 cytochrome bd-I 的膜亚基**

- **功能**：  
    cydB 编码的蛋白是 **细胞色素 bd-I 醌醇氧化酶**复合体的一部分，该复合体在低氧、NO 应激等环境高度表达，具有高亲和力 O₂ 利用能力，可使用醌醇为电子供体、氧或 NO 为电子受体。
    
- **沙门菌突变表型**：
    
    - cydAB/cydABX 缺失突变株在低氧和 NO 条件下生长和存活明显受限；
        
    - 在巨噬细胞内以及小鼠系统性感染模型中存活不佳，尤其在脾脏、肝脏等缺氧组织中竞争力明显下降；
        
    - 在肠道腔内的竞争殖方面影响较小，提示其主要对“宿主细胞内 / 组织内生存”重要。
        
- **毒力意义**：  
    cytochrome bd-I 已被视为一种 **毒力相关的呼吸因子**。抑制该复合体可削弱沙门菌在宏噬细胞内和炎症组织中的生存能力，因此不少工作将其作为新的抗感染靶点来探索。
    

**可引用文献（示例）：**

- Jones-Carson J et al., 2016, _Infection and Immunity_  
    “Cytochrome bd-dependent bioenergetics and _Salmonella_ survival in macrophages and during infection.”
    
- Duc KM et al., 2020, _Journal of Bacteriology_  
    “The small protein CydX is required for cytochrome bd quinol oxidase stability and function in _Salmonella enterica_ serovar Typhimurium.”
    
- Litvak Y et al., 2019, _Cell Host & Microbe_  
    探讨肠道肠杆菌科共栖菌如何保护宿主免受 _S. Typhimurium_ 侵袭，其中使用 cydAB 突变株进行肠道定植竞争实验。

### 3. **rfbB（rmlB）—— O 抗原 / ECA 的 dTDP-L-rhamnose 合成酶**

- **功能与结构**：  
    rfbB 编码 **dTDP-D-glucose 4,6-脱水酶**，在 dTDP-L-rhamnose 合成通路中充当第二步酶，是合成鼠李糖 O 抗原重复单元和 ECA 的关键。沙门菌 RmlB/RfbB 的晶体结构已被解析，活性位点和催化残基明确。
    
- **表型与 LPS 结构**：  
    在 S. Typhimurium 中，rfbB 与 rffG 双缺失造成：
    
    - O 抗原大幅度减少甚至缺失，菌落由光滑型转为粗糙型；
        
    - 细胞形态异常、外膜通透性升高、鞭毛运动受损；
        
    - 在小鼠口服或腹腔感染中，派伊尔集合淋巴结、脾、肝等组织的定植能力明显下降。
        
- **毒力与疫苗/药物开发**：  
    O 抗原和 ECA 是沙门菌外膜的重要结构，其缺陷会严重削弱细菌对血清、补体以及宿主细胞的抵抗能力。因此 rfbB 被认为是 **LPS 靶向药物和减毒活疫苗构建**中的有前景靶点。
    

**可引用文献（示例）：**

- Chakraborty S et al., 2025, _Frontiers in Microbiology_  
    “Functional loss of rffG and rfbB, encoding dTDP-glucose 4,6-dehydratase, alters colony morphology, cell shape, motility and virulence in _Salmonella Typhimurium_.”
    
- Allard STM et al., 2001, _Journal of Molecular Biology_  
    “The crystal structure of dTDP-D-glucose 4,6-dehydratase (RmlB) from _Salmonella enterica_.”

### 4. **lpxA——脂 A 合成首要酰基转移酶，通常为必需基因**

- **功能**：  
    lpxA 编码 **UDP-GlcNAc acyltransferase**，催化脂 A 合成通路中第一个专一性步骤：将 3-羟基酰-ACP 的酰基转移给 UDP-GlcNAc，生成 UDP-3-O-acyl-GlcNAc。该酶在革兰阴性菌中高度保守。
    
- **在沙门菌中的必需性与表型**：
    
    - 在 _S. Typhi_ 的高密度 Tn-seq 研究中，lpxA 所在的 operon（fabZ–lpxA–lpxB–rnhB–dnaE–accA）几乎没有可存活的插入突变，被归类为 **必需基因区域**；
        
    - 在 _S. Typhimurium_ 的药物靶点优选和宿主互作双转录组分析中，lpxA 被反复识别为与外膜稳态和感染适应性密切相关的核心基因。
        
- **毒力与药物靶点意义**：  
    脂 A 是 LPS 的内毒素部分，既是细菌外膜完整性的关键，又是宿主 TLR4 等受体识别的主要结构。
    
    - 部分研究表明，调节 lpxA 通路可改变细菌对多黏菌素等膜靶向抗生素的敏感性；
        
    - 抑制 LpxA 能显著削弱 LPS 产生，因此常被列为 **广谱抗革兰阴性药物设计的首要靶点之一**。
        

**可引用文献（示例）：**

- Langridge GC et al., 2009, _PLoS Genetics_  
    “Simultaneous assay of every _Salmonella Typhi_ gene using dense transposon mutagenesis.”
    
- Kocabaş K et al., 2022, _PLoS One_  
    “Dual transcriptome based reconstruction of _Salmonella_–host interactome.”
    
- Wyckoff TJ et al., 1998, _Journal of Biological Chemistry_  
    “Hydrocarbon rulers in UDP-N-acetylglucosamine acyltransferases of lipid A biosynthesis.”

### 5. **rfaD（hldD / waaD）—— ADP-L-heptose 差向异构酶**

- **功能与定位**：  
    rfaD 编码 **ADP-L-glycero-D-manno-heptose-6-epimerase**，将 ADP-D-heptose 差向异构为 ADP-L-heptose，是内核肝庚糖转移酶使用的直接供体。  
    在 **S. Typhimurium LT2** 中，rfaD 位于约 79 min 的 rfa 基因簇内（rfaDFCL），早期克隆和测序工作已经明确其在 LPS 内核合成中的定位。
    
- **突变表型**：
    
    - rfaD 突变株产生肝庚糖立体结构异常的 LPS，表现为 **deep-rough LPS**，对温度和外界应激更敏感；
        
    - 外膜通透性增加，对某些药物 / 抗菌因子敏感性改变；
        
    - 由于内核结构不完整，作为某些 H 群接合质粒的受体能力显著下降，说明 LPS 内核对细胞间接合接触也很重要。
        
- **与宿主免疫的关系**：  
    rfaD 与 gmhB 一起控制 ADP-L-heptose 的生成，而 ADP-heptose 已被证明能在宿主细胞胞浆中被 **ALPK1** 感知，引发下游炎症反应。因此，rfaD 不仅是 LPS 结构基因，也通过 ADP-heptose 代谢间接影响 **宿主先天免疫识别**。
    

**可引用文献（示例）：**

- Sirisena DM et al., 1994, _Journal of Bacteriology_  
    “Molecular analysis of the rfaD gene, for heptose synthesis, and the rfaF gene, for heptose transfer, in lipopolysaccharide synthesis in _Salmonella Typhimurium_.”
    
- Sherburne C & Taylor DE, 1997, _Journal of Bacteriology_  
    “Effect of lipopolysaccharide mutations on recipient ability of _Salmonella Typhimurium_ for incompatibility group H plasmids.”
    
- Coleman WG Jr., 1983, _Journal of Biological Chemistry_  
    “The rfaD gene codes for ADP-L-glycero-D-mannoheptose-6-epimerase, an enzyme required for lipopolysaccharide core biosynthesis.”（虽然是在大肠杆菌中完成，但酶与通路在沙门菌中高度保守。）

## English Review

**Title:**
**RfbABCD–LPS–Lipid A Axis in Salmonella and Other Gram-Negative Bacteria: From Cell Envelope Architecture to Virulence, Phage Resistance and Vaccines**

### 1. Overview: Why rfbABCD and lipid A matter

In Gram-negative bacteria, the cell envelope, especially lipopolysaccharide (LPS) and enterobacterial common antigen (ECA), is central to environmental survival, immune recognition, and virulence. In *Salmonella enterica*, the **rfb (O-antigen)** locus encodes the enzymes that synthesize dTDP-L-rhamnose, a key building block of many O-antigens and ECA. Classical genetic work in *S. Typhimurium* LT 2 showed that **rfbA, rfbB, rfbC, rfbD** form a conserved pathway converting glucose-1-phosphate into dTDP-L-rhamnose, and that these genes sit in an O-antigen operon with multiple additional glycosyltransferases and transport genes. ([PubMed][1])

Parallel studies in *Shigella flexneri*, *Leptospira interrogans*, *Xanthomonas campestris* and *Azorhizobium caulinodans* found **rfbCDBA-like clusters** required for rhamnose-containing LPS or surface polysaccharides, underlining the evolutionary conservation of this sugar pathway and its role in host interaction and environmental fitness. ([PubMed][2])

Downstream of LPS, the **lipid A** moiety is the main TLR 4 agonist. Its acylation pattern is determined by acyltransferases such as **LpxA and LpxD**, which are unusual hexapeptide-repeat enzymes conserved across many Gram-negative bacteria. ([科学直达][3]) Together, rfbABCD-dependent O-antigen/ECA and LpxA/D-dependent lipid A define the immunological “face” and the physical robustness of the Salmonella envelope.

Your collected references span from classical biochemistry to recent functional genomics, allowing a synthetic view that connects **(i)** envelope biosynthesis, **(ii)** host–pathogen interactions (autophagy, colonization, virulence), **(iii)** phage resistance and environmental persistence, and **(iv)** translational applications in vaccine design and anti-infective strategies.

---

### 2. Biochemistry and genetics of the dTDP-L-rhamnose pathway (rfbABCD)

Biochemical purification and cloning studies in *S. enterica* LT 2 defined the four canonical enzymes:

* **RfbA** – glucose-1-phosphate thymidylyltransferase (TDP-glucose synthetase), catalyzing the reversible formation of dTDP-D-glucose. ([ResearchGate][4])
* **RfbB** – dTDP-D-glucose 4,6-dehydratase. ([Frontiers][5])
* **RfbC** – dTDP-4-keto-6-deoxy-D-glucose 3,5-epimerase.
* **RfbD** – dTDP-L-rhamnose synthase (4-keto-reductase).

These four enzymes sequentially convert glucose-1-phosphate into dTDP-L-rhamnose, which is then transferred into O-antigen or ECA repeat units by specific rhamnosyltransferases. ([PMC][6])

The **rfb gene cluster** in *S. Typhimurium* includes not only rfbABCD but also several sugar transferase genes and a putative O-antigen flippase (rfbX), forming an operon whose GC content and codon usage suggest acquisition by horizontal gene transfer. ([PubMed][1]) This “LPS island” is a hotspot for variation, which underlies the enormous serovar diversity in *Salmonella* and other bacteria.

Cross-species comparisons (e.g. *S. flexneri, Leptospira, Xanthomonas, Azorhizobium*) reveal that rfbCDBA-like clusters can restore normal LPS in heterologous rfb-deficient mutants, confirming functional conservation of the pathway. ([PubMed][2])

---

### 3. RfbB and RffG: coupling O-antigen/ECA synthesis to morphology, motility and virulence

Recent work in *S. Typhimurium* has revisited RfbB in the context of its paralog **RffG** (both dTDP-glucose 4,6-dehydratases for O-antigen and ECA). Chakraborty et al. Generated ΔrfbB, ΔrffG and double ΔrfbBΔrffG mutants. ([Frontiers][5]) Key findings:

* Single mutants are relatively normal in rich medium, but the **double mutant has round cell morphology, small colonies, increased surface hydrophobicity, rapid auto-aggregation and hypersensitivity to bile and cell wall-targeting antibiotics**.
* Transcriptomics showed **strong down-regulation of flagellar and SPI-1 genes**, leading to defective swimming/swarming, adhesion and invasion.
* In mice, the double mutant was dramatically attenuated orally and intraperitoneally, with poor colonization of Peyer’s patches, spleen and liver and reduced induction of pro-inflammatory cytokines.

Earlier vaccine studies exploited this biology: a **regulated rfbB/rffG strain (S 496)** with arabinose-dependent O-antigen/ECA synthesis is fully attenuated but still colonizes mice and elicits strong antibody responses to conserved outer membrane proteins (OMPs), conferring cross-protection against heterologous *Salmonella* serovars. ([PubMed][7]) Similar more recent work deleting rfaL/rfbB/rffG/wzy also shows robust protection against homologous *S. Typhimurium* and partial protection against *S. Choleraesuis*. ([MDPI][8])

Together, these data support a model where **fine-tuned truncation of O-antigen/ECA** can:

1. Expose conserved OMPs → broaden protective immunity.
2. Attenuate systemic virulence → improved safety.
3. Alter colonization and tissue tropism, which must be balanced for live vaccines.

---

### 4. RfbD as a central node: autophagy, phage resistance and environmental survival

#### 4.1 RfbD suppresses autophagy and promotes intracellular survival

In *S. Enteritidis*, Zhou et al. Used a high-content screen of a transposon library and identified **rfbD** as a negative regulator of autophagy. Deletion of rfbD increased LC 3-II levels in HeLa and macrophage cell lines, decreased intracellular survival, and attenuated virulence in mice, with higher LC 3-II expression in liver and cecum. ([PMC][6]) Inhibiting autophagic flux rescued the survival defect of ΔrfbD, indicating that **RfbD helps the bacterium evade autophagic killing**, likely by altering LPS structure and its recognition.

Mechanistically, dTDP-L-rhamnose deficiency affects O-antigen and possibly ECA; changes in surface charge and patterning may modulate engagement of pattern-recognition receptors and autophagy pathways.

#### 4.2 RfbD-mediated phage resistance and fitness trade-offs

Two independent in vivo and in vitro studies have now linked **rfbD mutations to phage resistance** in *S. Enteritidis*:

* Zeng et al. Showed that a C→T nonsense mutation at position 520 of rfbD in a phage-exposed mutant caused **truncated LPS and resistance to a virulent phage (Psm 140)**; rfbD deletion or mutation prevented phage infection, whereas complementation restored susceptibility. However, rfbD mutants grew slower, sedimented faster, and were more sensitive to pH, temperature and antibiotics, indicating reduced environmental adaptability. ([PubMed][9])
* In broiler chickens treated with a six-phage cocktail, Agapé et al. Recovered *Salmonella* isolates with two adaptation profiles: a rough LPS phenotype with **rfbD mutations** conferring complete phage resistance, and a phase-variable phenotype with increased LPS glucosylation providing partial resistance. Both modifications impaired gut colonization to some extent. ([ResearchGate][4])

These studies show that **rfbD is a key phage receptor determinant**, but phage-resistant variants pay a fitness cost in colonization and environmental robustness—an evolutionary trade-off that could be harnessed in phage therapy design to deliberately push pathogens into less virulent states.

#### 4.3 RfbD and survival in low-moisture foods

Transposon-sequencing of *S. Enteritidis* and *S. Typhimurium* libraries on low-moisture pistachios identified **rfbD** among genes under strong negative selection, and ΔrfbD mutants showed impaired long-term survival on pistachios. ([VIVO][10]) Again, this underscores how **LPS O-antigen integrity is critical not only in vivo but also in dry-stress food environments**.

---

### 5. Rfb genes beyond Salmonella: Bacteroidota rfbA-typing and operon fragmentation

Moving to the gut microbiota, Bank and colleagues developed an **rfbA-based classification system** (“rfbA-typing”) for *Parabacteroides distasonis* and other Bacteroidetes. They showed that rfbA copy number and sequence variation stratify strains into distinct types and “superclusters”, and that **most pathogenic *P. distasonis* strains carry single-copy rfbA-Type I**, while other types are more associated with commensal/probiotic candidates. ([PubMed][11])

A companion study used the **Enterobacteriaceae rfbABCDX operon** as a model to examine rfb architecture across Bacteroidota. Most genomes displayed **fragmentation of the rfb operon into “mini-operons” and singlets**, often due to insertions of Bacteroides DNA within antigenic loci (rfb, fimbriae), but not within essential operons (ribosomal), suggesting relaxed selection and niche-driven diversification. ([PMC][12]) These fragmented operons often cannot produce full O-antigen and are associated with bacteria that elicit **relatively weak TNF-α responses and lack strong peritonitis potential** in mice, compared to Enterobacteriaceae from Crohn’s disease fistulas. ([PMC][12])

Together, this supports a broader concept: **operon integrity vs. Fragmentation of pro-inflammatory surface antigen loci** may be a genomic marker of commensalism vs. Opportunistic pathogenicity in gut bacteria.

---

### 6. Lipid A acyltransferases (LpxA/D) and the hexapeptide-repeat family

LpxA and LpxD catalyze the first acyl transfer steps in lipid A biosynthesis and exhibit unusual **tandem hexapeptide repeats** forming β-strand-rich structures. ([科学直达][3]) These repeats are highly conserved at specific positions (e.g. Ile/Leu/Val at position 1 of each hexad), suggesting structural constraints for acyl-donor recognition.

Functionally, mutations in lipid A biosynthesis (e.g. lpxA, firA/rfaE) or inner-core LPS can dramatically **increase susceptibility to hydrophobic antibiotics such as macrolides, rifampin and fusidic acid**, underlining the outer membrane’s barrier role. ([科学直达][13])

More recently, Yang et al. Engineered *S. Typhimurium* to express heterologous **lpxA or lpxD with altered acyl-chain length** (C 10 or C 16). C 10-containing lipid A reduced endotoxicity and colonization and increased polymyxin susceptibility, whereas C 16 modification in a vaccine backbone strain enhanced immunogenicity against *Streptococcus pneumoniae* with good protection, illustrating that **fine-tuning lipid A acyl chain length can decouple toxicity from vaccine efficacy**. ([科学直达][3])

These data position lipid A acyltransferases as attractive but delicate vaccine-engineering targets.

---

### 7. Cell envelope biosynthesis and susceptibility to anti-infectives

A 2025 study with the anti-infective small molecule **D 66** (kills *Salmonella* in macrophages and mice but not in rich medium) used forward genetics and Tn-seq and found spontaneous **gmhB mutations** (LPS inner-core heptose pathway) that conferred increased resistance to D 66 and other antibiotics, reflecting a tighter permeability barrier. Conversely, mutants in outer-membrane permeability or efflux pump genes were hypersusceptible to D 66. ([MDPI][14])

This reinforces the idea that **cell-envelope biosynthetic genes (gmhB, rfb, lpx)** shape susceptibility to non-traditional anti-infectives, not just classic antibiotics. Targeting these pathways may produce paradoxical phenotypes: some envelope mutants become less virulent but also more drug-tolerant.

---

### 8. Respiratory metabolism (cydB/cydX) and nitrosative stress

Beyond LPS, your reference list includes genes like **cydB and cydX**, subunits of cytochrome bd quinol oxidase. Cytochrome bd has high oxygen affinity and protects bacteria under low O 2 and nitrosative stress. The small protein **CydX** is now recognized as essential for cytochrome bd assembly and activity in *S. Typhimurium*; mutants lacking CydX phenocopy ΔcydABX, with defective oxidase activity, increased sensitivity to β-mercaptoethanol and nitric oxide, reduced proliferation in macrophages, but paradoxically increased resistance to some antibiotics. ([AMiner][15])

At the regulatory level, **RyhB-1/2 small RNAs** are induced by S-nitrosoglutathione and downregulate cyo, cydB and cybC while upregulating nirBCD, fine-tuning respiration and nitrite reduction under nitrosative stress. ([乌安第斯大学研究者][16]) These connections emphasize that **respiratory chain composition, LPS structure and stress responses are tightly coordinated**.

### 9. Classical virulence phenotypes: colonization, biofilm and serum resistance

Several older but still highly relevant studies from your list link rfb genes to pathogenesis:

* Tn 5 mutagenesis in *S. Typhimurium* identified **rfbK, rfbB, rfaK, rfaY** among genes required for colonization of the chicken gut and virulence in newly hatched chicks. ([ASM Journals][17])
* In DT 104, **rfbA mutants** show reduced biofilm formation and attachment to stainless steel and glass, likely due to altered LPS and flagella. ([OUCI][18])
* In *Neisseria meningitidis*, galE mutations that truncate LOS and prevent sialylation greatly reduce serum resistance, whereas rfbBAD homologs are largely cryptic, highlighting how **LPS core modifications and sialylation, rather than rhamnose pathway per se, dominate complement resistance** in that species. ([ResearchGate][19])

These studies collectively show that **rfb-dependent envelope structures impact colonization, serum resistance and biofilms in a species-specific manner**, modulated by the rest of the envelope and secreted virulence factors.

### 10. Translational implications and open questions

From your compiled literature and additional sources, several themes emerge:

1. **RfbABCD as a virulence/fitness hub**

   * Essential for full O-antigen/ECA and thus for motility, colonization, biofilm, and environmental survival.
   * At the same time, “controlled damage” (e.g. regulated rfbB/rffG expression) can attenuate virulence and unmask conserved antigens for vaccine design.

2. **RfbD as a strategic node in host and phage interaction**

   * Modulates autophagy evasion and intracellular survival in *S. Enteritidis*. ([PMC][6])
   * Serves as a major phage receptor target where mutations confer resistance but at the cost of growth, environmental resilience and gut colonization. ([PubMed][9])

3. **Lipid A acylation balance**

   * Fine-tuning acyl chain number/length through LpxA/D and other lipid A enzymes can reduce endotoxicity while preserving or enhancing vaccine immunogenicity, but may affect membrane stability and antimicrobial susceptibility. ([科学直达][3])

4. **Operon integrity vs fragmentation as a marker of pathogenic potential**

   * In Bacteroidota, fragmented rfb mini-operons correlate with attenuated pro-inflammatory potential and commensal behavior, offering a conceptual mirror to the intact, highly pro-inflammatory rfb operons of *Salmonella*. ([PubMed][11])

5. **Anti-infective strategies**

   * Small molecules like D 66 exploit envelope vulnerabilities and intracellular conditions; however, spontaneous mutations in LPS core pathways (gmhB, etc.) can tune permeability and drug susceptibility in complex ways. ([MDPI][14])
   * Phage therapy inevitably selects LPS variants (often rfbD mutants), and rational cocktails could be designed to push pathogens into attenuated yet phage-susceptible states, using fitness trade-offs to our advantage.

For your own work on *Salmonella Pullorum* vertical transmission and “double stealth” (PAMP masking + metabolic rewiring), these insights suggest several directions:

* Comparative genomics of **rfbABCD/rffG/gmhB/lpxA-D** across SP vs. *S. Enteritidis* and *S. Typhimurium* to see whether SP has evolved *restrained* or *re-wired* rhamnose/LPS pathways during host specialization.
* Testing whether **rfbD or gmhB variants** in SP influence phage susceptibility, egg transmission, or intracellular persistence in oviduct/ovary macrophages.
* Integrating **respiratory chain composition (cydABX)** and small RNA regulation (RyhB homologs) with LPS phenotypes in reproductive vs intestinal niches.

**题目：**
**Salmonella 及其他革兰阴性菌中 rfbABCD–LPS–Lipid A 轴：从细胞包膜结构到毒力、噬菌体耐受与疫苗设计**

### 1. 概述：为什么关注 rfbABCD 和 lipid A

在革兰阴性菌中，细胞包膜尤其是脂多糖（LPS）和肠杆菌共同抗原（ECA）决定了环境适应性、宿主免疫识别以及致病性。在 *Salmonella enterica* 中，**rfb（O 抗原）基因簇**编码的 dTDP-L-鼠李糖合成途径，是许多 O 抗原和 ECA 中鼠李糖残基的来源。早期在 *S. Typhimurium* LT 2 的研究表明，**rfbA、rfbB、rfbC、rfbD** 构成一个保守途径，将葡萄糖-1-磷逐步转化为 dTDP-L-鼠李糖，并与多种糖基转移酶、转运蛋白一起组成 O 抗原操纵子，其 GC 含量和密码子使用提示该区域可能为水平转移的“LPS 岛”。 ([PubMed][1])

在 *Shigella flexneri*、*Leptospira interrogans*、*Xanthomonas campestris* 和 *Azorhizobium caulinodans* 等多种细菌中也鉴定出 rfbCDBA-样基因簇，这些基因能在异源 rfb 缺失突变株中恢复正常 LPS 表型，说明 dTDP-L-鼠李糖途径在进化上高度保守，并与宿主互作和环境适应密切相关。 ([PubMed][2])

在 LPS 的脂质部分，**lipid A** 是主要的 TLR 4 激动剂，其酰链数目和长度由 **LpxA、LpxD 等酰基转移酶**决定。这类酶具有特殊的六肽重复结构，在多种细菌中高度保守。 ([科学直达][3]) 综合来看，以 rfbABCD-依赖的 O 抗原 / ECA 和 LpxA/D-依赖的 lipid A 为核心的“包膜轴”，共同定义了 Salmonella 的免疫表型和物理屏障特性。

### 2. DTDP-L-鼠李糖（rfbABCD）途径的生化与遗传基础

在 *S. enterica* LT 2 中，纯化和克隆研究明确了四个核心酶：

* **RfbA**：葡萄糖-1-磷胸苷转移酶（TDP-葡萄糖合成酶），催化可逆形成 dTDP-D-葡萄糖。 ([ResearchGate][4])
* **RfbB**：dTDP-D-葡萄糖 4,6-脱水酶。 ([Frontiers][5])
* **RfbC**：dTDP-4-酮-6-脱氧-D-葡萄糖 3,5-差向异构酶。
* **RfbD**：dTDP-L-鼠李糖合成酶（4-酮还原酶）。

四步反应依次将葡萄糖-1-磷转化为 dTDP-L-鼠李糖，随后被鼠李糖转移酶接入 O 抗原或 ECA 重复单元。 ([PMC][6])

*Salmonella* 的 **rfb 基因簇** 中还包含多个糖基转移酶、O 抗原翻转蛋白等，整体组成一个 O 抗原操纵子，GC 含量和密码子偏好与染色体其他区域不同，提示其具有复杂的进化来源和重组历史。 ([PubMed][1])

在 *Shigella*、*Leptospira*、*Xanthomonas*、*Azorhizobium* 等物种中，rfbCDBA-样簇能互补 rfb 缺失突变株并恢复 LPS，说明 dTDP-L-鼠李糖途径在不同物种之间功能高度可互换。 ([PubMed][2])

### 3. RfbB 与 RffG：将 O 抗原 / ECA 合成与形态、运动性和毒力耦联

在 *S. Typhimurium* 中，最新研究将视角扩展到 dTDP-葡萄糖 4,6-脱水酶的两个同源酶 **RfbB 与 RffG**。Chakraborty 等构建了 ΔrfbB、ΔrffG 及双缺失 ΔrfbBΔrffG 突变株，发现： ([Frontiers][5])

* 单缺失株在富营养培养基中生长接近野生型；
* 双缺失株出现**细胞变圆、小菌落、表面疏水性增加、快速自聚集**，并对胆盐和部分细胞壁靶向抗生素高度敏感；
* 转录组显示 **鞭毛和 SPI-1 相关基因显著下调**，导致游动和群体运动缺陷、黏附和侵袭能力下降；
* 小鼠感染中，双缺失株在口服和腹腔感染模型中均显著减毒，Peyer 斑、脾和肝脏定植能力弱，炎症因子诱导明显降低。

基于这一机制，疫苗研究利用 **可调控的 rfbB/rffG 表达株（如 S 496）**：在体外依赖阿拉伯糖表达 rfbB，从而实现 O 抗原和 ECA 的可控合成。该菌株在小鼠中完全减毒但仍可定植，并诱导对肠杆菌保守外膜蛋白（OMPs）的强烈免疫应答，对异源 *S. Choleraesuis* 与 *S. Enteritidis* 挑战具有交叉保护。 ([PubMed][7]) 此外，删除 rfaL/rfbB/rffG/wzy 等 LPS 合成相关基因构建的 *S. Typhimurium* 活疫苗候选株也显示，对同型 *S. Typhimurium* 可提供完全保护，对 *S. Choleraesuis* 提供部分保护。 ([MDPI][8])

总体上，**适度、可控的 O 抗原 / ECA 截短**可以：

1. 暴露保守 OMP → 增强广谱保护性；
2. 降低全身毒力 → 提高安全性；
3. 改变定植和组织趋向 → 需要在活疫苗设计中精细平衡。

### 4. RfbD 作为关键节点：自噬调控、噬菌体耐受与环境存活

#### 4.1 RfbD 抑制自噬，促进胞内生存

在 *S. Enteritidis* 中，Zhou 等利用高内涵筛选转座子库，发现 **rfbD 缺失可显著增强宿主自噬水平**。ΔrfbD 感染 HeLa 与巨噬细胞时 LC 3-II 表达升高、细胞内存活降低；在小鼠模型中 ΔrfbD 显著减毒，肝脏和盲肠细菌负荷下降并伴随 LC 3-II 增加。抑制自噬流可部分恢复 ΔrfbD 的存活缺陷，提示 **RfbD 通过改变 LPS 结构抑制自噬清除**，利于菌体胞内持留。 ([PMC][6])

#### 4.2 RfbD 介导噬菌体受体，同时带来适应性代价

两项独立研究在噬菌体压力下都观察到 **rfbD 突变介导的噬菌体耐受**：

* Zeng 等在一株经噬菌体 Psm 140 处理的 *S. Enteritidis* 突变株中发现 rfbD 第 520 位点 C→T 的无义突变，导致 LPS 截短和对 Psm 140 完全耐受。RfbD 删除或突变均使菌株失去噬菌体敏感性，回补 rfbD 则恢复裂解。然而，rfbD 突变株生长变慢、沉降速度加快，对 pH、温度和多种抗生素更加敏感，显示环境适应性下降。 ([PubMed][9])
* 在肉鸡体内多次投喂六价噬菌体组合后，Agapé 等从肠道分离的 *Salmonella* 中发现两类适应谱：一类为 **粗型 LPS 的 rfbD 突变株**，对相应噬菌体完全耐受；另一类通过 LPS 糖基化增加、产生可逆相变，从而获得部分耐受。两类变异均在一定程度上削弱肠道定植。 ([ResearchGate][4])

因此，**rfbD 既是噬菌体的重要受体决定子，也是耐受变异的首选靶点，但其突变往往以定植和环境适应为代价**。在噬菌体治疗策略中，有望利用这种“适应性权衡”将病原体推向低毒力状态。

#### 4.3 低水分食品中的存活

对 *S. Enteritidis* 与 *S. Typhimurium* 的 Tn-seq 竞争实验显示，在带壳开心果等低水分食品中长期存放时，**rfbD 插入突变的相对丰度显著下降，rfbD 整基因缺失的菌株在单独存活实验中明显劣势**，说明完整 O 抗原不仅对体内感染重要，也对干燥食品环境中的长期存活至关重要。 ([VIVO][10])

### 5. Salmonella 之外的 rfb：Bacteroidota 的 rfbA 分型与操纵子片段化

在肠道共生菌中，Bank 等基于 **rfbA（O 抗原合成基因）** 建立了 *Parabacteroides distasonis* 及其他 Bacteroidetes 的分类体系（rfbA-typing）。他们发现 rfbA 的拷贝数和序列变异可将菌株划分为多个类型和“超簇”，其中 **多数致病性 *P. distasonis* 菌株为单拷贝 rfbA-Type I**，而其他类型更常见于潜在益生或共生菌株。 ([PubMed][11])

后续研究以肠杆菌 rfbABCDX 操纵子为模型，在 Bacteroidota 中系统分析 rfb 基因结构，发现大多数 Bacteroidota 的 rfb 操纵子被打散为若干“迷你操纵子”和单基因簇，这些断裂多由 *Bacteroides* DNA 插入抗原相关操纵子（如 rfb、菌毛基因）导致，而必需操纵子（核糖体）很少被插入，提示这种碎片化与特定肠壁微环境中的自然选择有关。 ([PMC][12])

这些碎片化 rfb 操纵子的菌株往往无法合成完整 O 抗原，对巨噬细胞诱导 TNF-α 的能力较弱，在小鼠腹膜炎模型中也不易致超急性炎症，明显不同于来源于克罗恩病瘘管病灶的 Enterobacteriaceae。 ([PMC][12])

综合来看，**炎性表面抗原操纵子的“完整 vs 片段化”状态可能是肠道菌群中致炎潜能与共生倾向的重要基因组标志**。

### 6. Lipid A 酰基转移酶（LpxA/D）与六肽重复家族

LpxA 与 LpxD 催化 lipid A 合成的前两步酰基转移反应，其蛋白结构由大量 **I/L/V-起始的六肽重复单元**构成，二级结构以 β-折叠为主，是一类非常独特的酰基转移酶家族。 ([科学直达][3])

功能上，lipid A 合成（如 lpxA、firA/rfaE）或 LPS 内核相关突变可显著 **提高对大环内酯、利福平、夫西地酸等疏水抗生素的敏感性**，强调完整外膜对这些药物的屏障作用。 ([科学直达][13])

Yang 等通过引入异源 **lpxA 或 lpxD**，分别构建了具有 C 10 或 C 16 酰链的 lipid A 变体。C 10 变体显示多黏菌素敏感性增加、游动和侵袭能力下降、内毒性和定植能力降低，可作为 detox 化策略；而将 C 16 修饰引入现有 RASV 骨架后，仍保持良好安全性，同时显著增强对肺炎链球菌抗原的免疫应答和保护力。 ([科学直达][3]) 这表明 **精细调控 lipid A 酰链长度有望在“降低毒性”和“保持/增强免疫原性”之间取得平衡**。

### 7. 细胞包膜生物合成与“抗感染小分子”的敏感性

2025 年关于抗感染小分子 **D 66** 的研究表明，该化合物在巨噬细胞和小鼠组织中可高效杀灭 *S. Typhimurium*，但在常规培养条件下无抑菌活性。正向遗传筛选发现自发 **gmhB（LPS 内核庚糖途径）点突变**可显著提高对 D 66 以及多种抗生素的耐受，推测与外膜通透性降低有关；相反，外膜通透性或外排泵相关基因插入突变则明显降低在 D 66 存在下的适应度。 ([MDPI][14])

这提示 **包膜生物合成基因（gmhB、rfb、lpx 等）不仅影响经典抗生素敏感性，也决定非传统“抗感染小分子”的作用窗**。针对这些途径设计药物时，既可能获得低毒、低耐药的优点，也要警惕出现“减毒但耐药”的复杂表型。

### 8. 呼吸代谢（cydB/cydX）与硝基应激

你的文献列表还涉及 **cydB 和 cydX** 等细胞色素 bd 复合体亚基。Cytochrome bd 具有高氧亲和力，在低氧和强硝基应激环境中保护细菌呼吸。最新研究表明，小蛋白 **CydX** 对 cytochrome bd 的装配和功能至关重要；缺失 CydX 的 *S. Typhimurium* 在光谱学和酶活上接近 ΔcydABX，表现为 β-巯基乙醇和一氧化氮敏感性增加、巨噬细胞内增殖受限，但对部分抗生素耐受性反而提高。 ([AMiner][15])

在调控层面，**RyhB-1/2 小 RNA** 在 S-亚硝基谷胱甘肽（GSNO）诱导下表达上调，抑制 cyo、cydB、cybC 等细胞色素氧化酶基因的表达，同时促进 nirBCD（亚硝酸盐还原系统），从而在硝基应激下精细调节呼吸链构成。 ([乌安第斯大学研究者][16])

这些发现强调：**呼吸链组成、LPS 结构与宿主氧化/硝基应激反应是一个高度耦联的网络**，对 Salmonella 在肠道和生殖道等低氧环境中的适应至关重要。

### 9. 经典毒力表型：定植、生物被膜与血清耐受

早期多项研究为我们提供了 rfb 与毒力之间联系的直接证据：

* 在 *S. Typhimurium* F 98 的 Tn 5 突变库中，**rfbK、rfbB、rfaK、rfaY** 等 LPS 相关基因被证明对 3 周龄鸡肠道定植和 1 日龄雏鸡毒力至关重要。 ([ASM Journals][17])
* 在耐药流行株 DT 104 中，**rfbA 插入突变**导致生物被膜形成能力下降，在不锈钢和玻璃表面附着显著减少，提示 O 抗原与鞭毛、胞外多糖共同决定食品加工环境中的持留。 ([OUCI][18])
* 在 *Neisseria meningitidis* 中，galE 突变造成 LOS 截短和失去唾液酸化，血清耐受性明显下降；而 cps 区内的 rfbBAD 同源基因基本处于静默状态，对 LOS 表型影响不大，说明在该菌中 **内核结构和唾液酸化，而非鼠李糖途径本身，是补体抵抗的关键**。 ([ResearchGate][19])

这些结果共同表明，**rfb 相关包膜结构对定植、血清耐受和生物被膜形成有重要影响，但其具体作用受物种背景和其他包膜因子调节**。

### 10. 结语：对你 Pullorum 课题的启示与开放问题

综合你的文献整理与补充检索，可以概括出几个关键观点：

1. **rfbABCD 是包膜结构与致病性的“枢纽”**

   * 保证完整 O 抗原 / ECA，从而影响鞭毛运动、定植、生物被膜及低水分环境存活；
   * 适度截短（如可调控 rfbB/rffG）既可减毒，又可暴露保守抗原，是活疫苗设计的有力工具。

2. **rfbD 在宿主防御与噬菌体压力之间充当关键节点**

   * 通过改变 LPS 结构抑制自噬清除，促进 *S. Enteritidis* 的胞内存活与毒力。 ([PMC][6])
   * 又是多种噬菌体的主要受体，点突变或缺失可赋予强耐受，但伴随生长变慢、环境与肠道适应性下降。 ([PubMed][9])

3. **lipid A 酰链精细调控的双刃剑效应**

   * 通过 LpxA/D 等调节酰链数目与长度，可减少内毒活性，同时维持或增强疫苗免疫原性，但也可能影响膜稳定性和抗菌药敏。 ([科学直达][3])

4. **抗原操纵子的完整度作为致炎潜能标志**

   * Bacteroidota 中 rfb 操纵子的广泛片段化，与其低 TNF-α 诱导和共生倾向相吻合，为理解肠道共生 / 条件致病平衡提供了新的基因组视角。 ([PubMed][11])

5. **面向疗法：小分子与噬菌体的“进化利用”**

   * D 66 之类小分子利用包膜脆弱性和胞内环境发挥作用，但 gmhB 等突变可能产生“减毒但更耐药”的复杂结果。 ([MDPI][14])
   * 噬菌体治疗必然驱动 LPS / rfb 变异，合理组合噬菌体可引导病原体向低毒、高成本的耐受状态演化，从而在控制细菌数量的同时削弱其致病性。

对于你正在构建的“鸡白痢沙门菌双重隐身 + 代谢重编 → 垂直传播适应性进化”课题，这些结果提示可以重点考虑：

* 比较 *S. Pullorum* 与 *S. Enteritidis*、*S. Typhimurium* 的 **rfbABCD/rffG/gmhB/lpxA-D** 变异，分析 SP 是否通过“有限 O 抗原 / ECA”或特殊 lipid A 修饰实现“低炎症 + 持久定植”；
* 检测 SP 中 **rfbD 或 gmhB 变异** 是否会影响噬菌体敏感性、卵传递效率或卵巢 / 输卵管巨噬细胞中的持久感染；
* 把 **呼吸链（cydABX）和小 RNA（RyhB 同源物）调控** 纳入生殖道与肠道双环境的比较框架，构建“包膜–代谢–免疫”一体化模型。
