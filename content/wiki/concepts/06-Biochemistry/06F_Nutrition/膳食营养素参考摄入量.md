- EAR（estimated average requirement) 估计平均需要量
- RNI (recommended nutrient intake) 推荐摄入量
- AI (adequate intake) 适宜摄入量
- UL (tolerable upper intake level) 可耐受最高摄入量
- NOAEL (No observed Adverse Effect Level) 无可见有害作用水平
- LOAEL (Lowest Observed Adverse Intake Level) 最小可见有害作用水平
- AMDR（acceptable macronutrient distribution ranges) 宏量营养素可接受范围
- PI-NCD（proposed intakes for Preventing Non-Communicable Chronic Disease) 预防非传染性慢性疾病的建议摄入量
- SPL (specific proposed levels) 特定建议值
